## 1. Title

**Dash2Frontier**

## 2. Game home page

The home page shows the title, a short field briefing, a call-sign field, Create Room, and a five-digit numeric room-code join field. It also previews the four skills: Q Build, E Blast, R Overdrive, and F Cryo.

## 3. Game play page

The play page shows both miners' energy, nearest-gate progress, buffs, the timer, and a shared 72×28 mine. Players move and auto-drill the block they face; while turning switches the target. Different blocks take different times, and bedrock forces detours. Q/E/F are mouse-aimed; R is instant. The first player to enter the rival's upper or lower gate wins. If time expires, the miner closest to a gate wins; an exact tie is a draw.

## 4. Game over page

The game-over overlay shows Victory, Defeat, or Draw, the reason, the gate used, match duration, and both players' stats. Players can Rematch or Leave Room; readiness and offline status are shown.

## 5. Cheating

Triple-click **C** to enable. When enabled, instant mining, bedrock-breaking, and a timed rival freeze all activate together. Click **C** can disable it at any time.
