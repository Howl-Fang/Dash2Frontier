Field briefing

D2F / 04:00
  1. 01
    Two miners, one map. You start on opposite sides of the same block-filled seam.
  2. 02
    Move continuously with WASD or arrows. The block you face is drilled automatically. Let go and the drill keeps working; turn away to switch targets or walk off mid-dig. Bedrock cannot be broken, so plan a detour.
  3. 03
    Aim skills with the mouse. Drag barricades around the rival, blast a 3×3 hole, or drop a delayed frost burst. Overdrive is instant. Empty tunnel is a free walk.
  4. 04
    Breach an upper or lower gate. Only the two glowing stretches at the top and bottom of the rival baseline score. The middle is sealed and the spawn area is not a target. If the clock hits zero, the miner nearest a gate takes it.
